# Credits

Blanky stands on the work of people who shared it openly.
Thank you.

## Inspiration

- [Blanket](https://github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket) by Rafael Mardojai CM, the original ambient sound app for Linux.
- [Blankie](https://github.com/codybrom/Blankie) by Cody Bromley, for Mac, iPhone and iPad.
  Blanky's features, loop cuts, loudness levels and website design follow Blankie (MIT).
  The Blankie name and icons are Cody Bromley's trademarks and are not used here.
